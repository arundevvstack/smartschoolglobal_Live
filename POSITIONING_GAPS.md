# Competitive Positioning Gaps

This document identifies business and product evidence required from Smart School AI Global Pvt. Ltd. to strengthen Middle East market differentiation.

We cannot fabricate this information. The business must supply it.

## 1. Regional Compliance & Security
* **Gap:** Middle East CIOs require explicit data residency answers (e.g., UAE National Data Privacy Law, KSA PDPL).
* **Needed Evidence:** Documentation on AWS/Azure ME regions used, local data compliance certifications, and disaster recovery locations.

## 2. Integration Ecosystem
* **Gap:** IT Directors need to know how the system connects to existing legacy systems.
* **Needed Evidence:** A verified list of native integrations (e.g., Azure Active Directory, Google Workspace, Canvas, Moodle, specialized regional HR systems).

## 3. Financial Infrastructure
* **Gap:** Only global gateways (Stripe, PayPal) are mentioned. Schools need local context.
* **Needed Evidence:** Verified support for Middle East payment gateways (e.g., PayTabs, Tap Payments, Network International, STC Pay).

## 4. Trust & Social Proof
* **Gap:** The removal of fabricated testimonials leaves the site relying solely on aggregate metrics (300+ institutions).
* **Needed Evidence:** Authentic, named customer quotes. High-resolution logos of partner schools. Video testimonials.

## 5. Implementation & Support
* **Gap:** Replacing a school's core ERP is terrifying for management.
* **Needed Evidence:** An explanation of the onboarding methodology, timeline, and whether 24/7 localized (Arabic/English) support is provided.
