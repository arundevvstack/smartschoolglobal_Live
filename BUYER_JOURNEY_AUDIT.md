# Middle East Buyer Journey Audit

## 1. School Owner / Education Group Leader
* **WHAT DO THEY SEE FIRST?** "Building Smarter Schools, With AI."
* **WHAT PROBLEM DO THEY UNDERSTAND?** Administrative burden and disconnected systems.
* **WHAT PRODUCT CAPABILITY DO THEY DISCOVER?** Multi-campus command-and-control, 68% workload reduction.
* **WHAT PROOF DO THEY SEE?** 300+ institutions, 20+ countries.
* **WHAT OBJECTION REMAINS?** "Is my data secure and regionally compliant (e.g., UAE/KSA data residency)?"
* **WHAT CTA DO THEY GET?** "Speak to Sales" / "Book a Demo"
* **WHAT WOULD MAKE THEM BOOK A DEMO?** Seeing a clear ROI case study (which is currently missing).

## 2. Principal / Head of School
* **WHAT DO THEY SEE FIRST?** Homepage.
* **WHAT PROBLEM DO THEY UNDERSTAND?** Teachers wasting time on admin.
* **WHAT PRODUCT CAPABILITY DO THEY DISCOVER?** Attendance, Timetable generation, Parent portal.
* **WHAT PROOF DO THEY SEE?** 3.5 hours saved per teacher.
* **WHAT OBJECTION REMAINS?** "How hard is it to train my teachers on this?" (Adoption friction is not addressed).
* **WHAT CTA DO THEY GET?** Book Demo.
* **WHAT WOULD MAKE THEM BOOK A DEMO?** Knowing that the UI is genuinely easy to use.

## 3. CIO / IT Director
* **WHAT DO THEY SEE FIRST?** Platform Overview.
* **WHAT PROBLEM DO THEY UNDERSTAND?** Fragmented legacy systems.
* **WHAT PRODUCT CAPABILITY DO THEY DISCOVER?** Unified database, native iOS/Android apps.
* **WHAT PROOF DO THEY SEE?** ISO 27001, 99.9% SLA.
* **WHAT OBJECTION REMAINS?** "How does it integrate with our existing active directory or LMS?" (Integration details are extremely light).
* **WHAT CTA DO THEY GET?** Book Demo.
* **WHAT WOULD MAKE THEM BOOK A DEMO?** API documentation or a technical architecture whitepaper.

## 4. Finance Leader
* **WHAT DO THEY SEE FIRST?** Fees module.
* **WHAT PROBLEM DO THEY UNDERSTAND?** Late payments and poor cash flow.
* **WHAT PRODUCT CAPABILITY DO THEY DISCOVER?** Predictive fee collection, automated reminders.
* **WHAT PROOF DO THEY SEE?** 94% fee collection rate.
* **WHAT OBJECTION REMAINS?** "What local ME payment gateways are supported?" (Only Stripe/PayPal are explicitly named).
* **WHAT CTA DO THEY GET?** Book Demo.
* **WHAT WOULD MAKE THEM BOOK A DEMO?** Confidence in local banking integration.

## Gaps Identified
1. Regional compliance (Data residency in UAE/KSA) is not explicitly answered.
2. Local payment gateway specifics are missing.
3. Implementation timeline and adoption support are not explained.
4. Missing technical integration documentation for IT decision makers.
