# Phase 4: Buyer Simulation

This document evaluates the website from the perspective of key Middle East education decision-makers.

### 1. School Owner
* **WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT:** ROI, business growth, brand reputation, competitive advantage, operational efficiency.
* **WHAT THEY SEE:** "Building Smarter Schools, With AI", 68% admin workload reduction, 94% fee collection rate.
* **WHAT THEY UNDERSTAND:** It's an AI-driven platform that handles the whole school and improves financial/operational metrics.
* **WHAT THEY STILL DON'T KNOW:** How much does it cost? Is it compliant with local UAE/KSA data regulations?
* **WHAT OBJECTION THEY MAY HAVE:** "Is this just a generic software repacked as AI?" or "Will it disrupt my current operations during transition?"
* **WHAT PROOF THEY SEE:** 300+ institutions, 94% fee collection rate.
* **WHAT CTA THEY RECEIVE:** Book a Demo.

### 2. Principal
* **WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT:** Academic outcomes, teacher retention, parent satisfaction, student safety.
* **WHAT THEY SEE:** 3.5 hours saved per teacher per week, 3x earlier identification of at-risk students, 60% improvement in parent engagement.
* **WHAT THEY UNDERSTAND:** The platform reduces teacher burnout and provides academic early warning systems.
* **WHAT THEY STILL DON'T KNOW:** Does the timetable generator actually handle complex curriculum requirements (e.g., IB/British/CBSE)?
* **WHAT OBJECTION THEY MAY HAVE:** "My teachers hate learning new software."
* **WHAT PROOF THEY SEE:** 3.5 hours saved metric, intuitive conceptual UI mockups.
* **WHAT CTA THEY RECEIVE:** Explore Features.

### 3. CIO / IT Director
* **WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT:** Security, data migration, integration, uptime, support, compliance.
* **WHAT THEY SEE:** ISO 27001 Certified, 99.9% Uptime SLA.
* **WHAT THEY UNDERSTAND:** It's a cloud-based SaaS platform with standard enterprise security.
* **WHAT THEY STILL DON'T KNOW:** Is there an open API? Does it integrate with MS Teams/Google Workspace? Where is the data hosted?
* **WHAT OBJECTION THEY MAY HAVE:** "We already have legacy systems; migration will be a nightmare."
* **WHAT PROOF THEY SEE:** Security certifications.
* **WHAT CTA THEY RECEIVE:** Explore Solutions.
