# Phase 4: Objection & Content Gaps

This document identifies unanswered questions a serious buyer would have, flagging them as CONTENT GAPs for the business team to resolve.

### 1. Data Residency & Compliance
**Objection:** "Where is our student data stored? Is it compliant with UAE PDPL or KSA PDPL?"
**Status:** CONTENT GAP
**Needed:** Explicit documentation on Middle East server regions (e.g., AWS ME-South) and local regulatory compliance.

### 2. Integrations & API
**Objection:** "How does it fit into our existing workflows? Do you integrate with Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, or our local government portals (e.g., KHDA/SPEA)?"
**Status:** CONTENT GAP
**Needed:** A verified list of supported third-party integrations and confirmation of government API sync capabilities.

### 3. Payment Gateways
**Objection:** "Can parents pay in AED/SAR through local providers?"
**Status:** CONTENT GAP
**Needed:** Verified list of supported Middle East payment gateways (e.g., Payfort, Telr, Moyasar, Tap Payments).

### 4. Implementation & Migration
**Objection:** "What happens to our existing data? How long does implementation take?"
**Status:** CONTENT GAP
**Needed:** A structured onboarding timeline, data migration SLA, and description of the customer success process.

### 5. Curriculum Support
**Objection:** "Does the timetable and grading system support IB, British (IGCSE), and local Ministry of Education curricula?"
**Status:** CONTENT GAP
**Needed:** Explicit confirmation of multi-curriculum support and custom rubric capabilities.

### 6. Transparent Pricing
**Objection:** "How does pricing work? What are the hidden fees?"
**Status:** CONTENT GAP
**Needed:** While exact pricing is custom, a transparent description of the *pricing model* (e.g., per student, per campus) and whether implementation/support is included.

### 7. Verified Social Proof
**Objection:** "Who else in our region uses this?"
**Status:** CONTENT GAP
**Needed:** Genuine, verified customer logos, case studies, or testimonials from institutions within the target region.
